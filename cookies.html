Cookies Policy

This policy explains how Braxelle Design Lines uses cookies and similar technologies, what they do, and how you can control them.

Last updated: 15 January 2025

This Cookies Policy describes how Braxelle Design Lines ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies, local storage, and similar technologies on our website at braxelledesignlines.com. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle personal information more broadly.

In short: We use a minimal set of cookies — mostly for basic site functionality and privacy-respecting analytics. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking cookies. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to the site owners about how the site is being used.

Cookies can be "session cookies" (which are deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (which remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them). They can also be "first-party cookies" (set by the site you are visiting) or "third-party cookies" (set by a domain other than the one you are visiting).

In addition to cookies, we may use other similar technologies such as local storage and session storage, which store information in your browser without sending it back to a server with every request.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies for the following limited purposes:

Strictly necessary cookies

These are essential for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility. Without these cookies, the site may not work correctly. These do not require your consent because they are necessary to provide the service you have requested.

Preference cookies

These cookies allow the site to remember choices you have made, such as whether you have dismissed a notice or banner. They help provide a more personalized experience and avoid you having to re-enter preferences each time you visit.

Analytics cookies

We use a privacy-respecting analytics service to understand how readers use our site — which articles are popular, how visitors navigate between pages, and where readers come from. This information is used in aggregate and does not identify you personally. We do not use analytics cookies to track you across other websites.

No advertising or tracking cookies

We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting cookies, or any cookies that track your browsing activity across other websites. We do not sell or share cookie data with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We do not set third-party advertising or tracking cookies. However, some pages may include embedded content (such as a YouTube video or a social media post) that could set cookies from those third-party services. We do not control those cookies, and they are governed by the privacy policies of the respective platforms. We encourage you to review their policies if you interact with such content.

Our analytics provider is contractually bound to use the data only for the purpose of providing analytics to us, and to process it in accordance with applicable data protection law.

5. How to Manage Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences by:

  • Browser settings — Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically find these options in the "Privacy" or "Security" section of your browser's settings menu. You can also delete cookies that have already been set.
  • Opt-out tools — Some third-party services provide opt-out mechanisms. For our analytics provider, you can typically opt out via their website or by using browser-based opt-out tools.
  • Device settings — On mobile devices, you may have additional controls over tracking through your device's privacy settings.

Please note that if you disable or delete cookies, some parts of the website may not function properly, and your preferences may be lost.

Browser help: For more information about how to manage cookies, you can visit allaboutcookies.org or consult the help documentation for your specific browser.

6. Your Consent

When you first visit our site, you may see a notice about our use of cookies. By continuing to use our site after that notice, or by clicking "Accept" where applicable, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting cookies from your browser and adjusting your browser settings to block them in the future.

Where required by law, we will obtain your explicit consent before setting any non-essential cookies. Strictly necessary cookies do not require consent as they are essential to provide the service you have requested.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, provide a notice on our homepage. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.
