Pitch Guidelines

  • We publish long-form articles (1,500–3,500 words) and practical guides.
  • Topics should fit our editorial focus: interior design, residential architecture, materials, lighting, and space planning.
  • Include a working title, a 150-word outline, and a link to a recent writing sample.
  • We do not accept sponsored content or paid placements.
  • Response time for pitches is typically 5–7 business days.

About Braxelle Design Lines

Braxelle Design Lines is an independent editorial publication devoted to interior design and architecture. We publish long-form articles, practical guides, and critical essays for people who care about how buildings and interiors are made—and why they matter.

Our editorial approach is grounded in research, craftsmanship, and a belief that good design writing should be both accessible and rigorous. We do not publish sponsored content, and we do not accept payment for coverage.
