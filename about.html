Design writing that respects the reader.

Braxelle Design Lines is an independent editorial publication devoted to interior design and architecture. We publish long-form articles, practical guides, and critical essays for people who care about how buildings and interiors are made—and why they matter.

Our mission

We believe that architecture and interior design are not luxuries or mere aesthetics—they are the backdrop of daily life, shaping how we feel, how we gather, and how we inhabit the world. Good design writing should be accessible without being shallow, rigorous without being academic, and useful long after the latest trend has passed.

Founded in 2019, Braxelle Design Lines exists to fill a gap: a publication that treats the built environment with intellectual seriousness while remaining clear, practical, and free of jargon. We write for homeowners, designers, architects, students, and anyone who has ever paused in a well-proportioned room and wondered why it felt right.

We are independent, reader-supported, and committed to publishing work that stands the test of time.

What we stand for

Independence

We do not publish sponsored content, and we do not accept payment for coverage. Our editorial decisions are made solely by our editors, in the interest of our readers.

Depth over speed

We would rather publish one carefully researched article a week than chase the news cycle. Every piece is edited for clarity, accuracy, and lasting relevance.

Practical knowledge

Design writing should help you make better decisions. Our guides are grounded in real-world constraints: budgets, building codes, climate, and the messy realities of construction.

Materials & craft

We are fascinated by how things are made. From lime plaster to cross-laminated timber, we explore the substances and skills that give buildings their character and longevity.

Historical perspective

Contemporary design does not exist in a vacuum. We trace ideas back to their origins—the courtyard, the kitchen triangle, the bay window—to understand where we are and where we might go.

Reader trust

We collect minimal data, we do not sell personal information, and we clearly disclose our policies. Our relationship with readers is built on transparency.

Editorial team

Marguerite Ellison

Editor-in-Chief

Marguerite has spent twenty years writing about the built environment. She founded Braxelle Design Lines in 2019 to create a publication that treats architecture and interior design with the intellectual seriousness they deserve—without sacrificing clarity or usefulness. She lives in Portland, Oregon, in a 1920s bungalow she is slowly, carefully renovating.

Daniel Kovač

Architecture Editor

Daniel is a writer and former practicing architect. He holds a Master of Architecture from the University of Ljubljana and has contributed to architectural journals across Europe and North America. His interests include adaptive reuse, material innovation, and the intersection of housing policy and design. He joined Braxelle in 2021.

Editorial policy

Braxelle Design Lines is committed to publishing accurate, original, and useful writing. Our editorial policy governs how we work, what we publish, and how we handle corrections.

  • Original work: Every article we publish is written by a member of our editorial team or a commissioned contributor. We do not use AI-generated content.
  • No sponsored content: We do not accept payment, gifts, or free products in exchange for coverage. Brands and products are mentioned only when editorially relevant.
  • Corrections: When we make an error, we correct it promptly and transparently. Substantive corrections are noted at the bottom of the article.
  • Independence: Our editorial team operates independently of any commercial or advertising interests. Braxelle Design Lines is reader-supported through newsletter subscriptions and affiliate partnerships that do not influence coverage.
  • Privacy: We collect only the data necessary to operate the site and communicate with readers. We do not sell or rent personal information. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Get in touch

We welcome editorial inquiries, story pitches, and feedback from readers. Whether you have a question about a project, a suggestion for a topic, or a correction to report, we read every message.
